1 sa jayati saṃkalpabhavo ratimukhaśatapatracumbanabhramaraḥ | yasyānuraktalalanānayanāntavilokitaṃ vasatiḥ
2 avadhīrya doṣanicayaṃ guṇaleśe saṃniveśya matimāryāḥ | kuṭṭanyā matametaddāmodaraguptaviracitaṃ śṛṇuta
3 asti khalu nikhilabhūtalabhūṣaṇabhūtā vibhūtiguṇayuktā | yuktābhiyuktajanatā nagarī vārāṇasī nāma
4 anubhavatāmapi yasyāmupabhogān kāmataḥ śarīravatām | śaśadharakhaṇḍavibhūṣitadehalayaḥ kila na duṣprāpaḥ
5 candravibhūṣitadehā bhūtiratāḥ sadbhajaṅgaparivārāḥ | vārastriyo 'pi yasyāṃ paśupatitanutulyatāṃ yātāḥ
6 atituṅgasuraniketanaśikharasamutkṣiptapavanacalitābhiḥ | mañjaritamiva virājati yattra nabho vaijayantībhiḥ
7 aviratasaṃcaradabalā-caraṇatalālaktakadravāruṇitam | sthalakamalavatīṃ lakṣmīṃ bibharti vasudhātalaṃ yattra
8 yatra ca ramaṇībhūṣaṇaravabadhiritasakaladiṅanabhobhāge | śiṣyāṇāṃ nācāryairavadyamavadhāryate paṭhatām
9 vindhyadharādharabhūriva yā rājati mattavāraṇopetā | bahulaniśīthavatīva projjvaladhiṣṇyopaśobhitā yā ca
10 yatigaraṇaguṇasamupetā yā nityaṃ chandasāmiva pracitiḥ | vanapaṅkiriva sasālā turuṣkaseneva bahalagandharvā
11 tārāgaṇo 'kulīnaḥ priyadoṣā yatra kauśikāḥ satatam | gadye vṛttacyavanaṃ paragṛharodhastathāceṣu
12 śūlabhṛto vyālāsthā padavediṣu yatra dhātuvāditvam | surateṣvabalākramaṇaṃ dānacchedo madacyutau kariṇām
13 tīvrakaratvaṃ bhānoraviveko yatra mivahṛdayānām | yogiṣu daṇḍagrahaṇaṃ saṃdhicchedaḥ pragṛhyeṣu
14 chandaḥprastāravidhau guravo yasyāmanārjavasthitayaḥ | vīṇāyāṃ parivādo dvijanilayeṣvaprasannatvam
15 anurūpavṛttaghaṭanā satkavikṛtarūpakeṣu loke ca | ramaṇīvacane yasyāṃ mādhuryaṃ kāvyabandhe ca
16 yasyāmupavanavīthyāṃ tamālapattrāṇi yuvativadane ca | nakharaprahārakaṇitaṃ tantrīvādye ca suratakalahe ca
17 nandanavanābhirāmā vibudhavatī nākavāhinījuṣṭā | amarāvatīva yānyā viśvasṛjā nirmitā jagati
18 tasyāṃ khagapatitanuriva vilāsināṃ hṛdayaśokasaṃjananī | ākṛṣṭeśvarahṛdayā prāleyanagādhirājatanayeva
19 saṃsaktabhoginevā mandaradharaṇībhūto yathā mūrtiḥ | uparigatā śūlānāmandhāsuragātralekheva
20 samuvāsa vārarāmā mānasavasateḥ śarīriṇī śaktiḥ | niḥśeṣaveśayoṣidvibhūṣaṇaṃ mālatī nāma
21 peśalavacasāṃ vasatirlīlānāmālayaḥ sthitiḥ premnaḥ | bhūmiḥ parihāsānāmāvasatho vakrakathitānām
22 sā śuśrāva kadā ciddhavalālayapṛṣṭhadeśamadhirūḍhā | kenāpi gīyamānāṃ prasaṅgapatitāmimāmāryām
23 yauvanasaundaryamadaṃ dūreṇāpāsya vāravanitābhiḥ | yatnena veditavyāḥ kāmukahṛdayārjanopāyāḥ
24 śrutvātha vipulajaghanā manasīdaṃ mālatī cakāra ciram | atisāmpratamupadiṣṭaṃ suhṛdevānena sādhunā paṭhatā
25 tadgatvā pṛcchāmo vikarālāṃ kalitasakalasaṃsārām | yasyāḥ kāmijanaugho divāniśaṃ dvāradeśamadhyāste
26 iti manasi sā niveśya drutataramavatīrya veśmanaḥ śikharāt | vikarālābhavanavaraṃ parijanaparivāritā prayayau
27 atha viralonnatadaśanāṃ nimnahaṃ sthūlacipiṭanāsāgrām | ulbaṇacūcukalakṣitaśuṣkakucasthānaśithilakṛttitanum
28 gambhīrāraktadṛśaṃ nirbhūṣaṇalambakarṇapālīṃ ca | katipayapāṇḍuracikurāṃ prakaṭaśirāsaṃtatāyatagrīvām
29 sitadhautavasanayugalāṃ vividhauṣadhimaṇisanāthagalasūtrām | tanvīmaṅgulimūle tapanīyamayīṃ ca vālikāṃ dadhatīm
30 gaṇikāgaraṇaparikaritāṃ kāmijanopāyanaprasaktadṛśam | āsandyāmāsīnāṃ vilokayāmāsa vikarālām
31 avalokya sā vidhāya kṣitimaṇḍalalīnamaulinā praṇatim | paripṛṣṭakuśalavārtā samanujñātāsanaṃ bheje
32 atha viracitahastapuṭā sapraśrayamāsanaṃ samutsṛjya | idamūce vikarālāmavasaramāsādya mālatī vacanam
33 vidadhāsi harimakaustubhamahari ravimagajanāthamamarendram | adraviṇaṃ draviṇapatitaṃ niyataṃ matigocare patitam
34 ayameva buddhivibhavaṃ hṛtavibhavaste paṭaccarāvaraṇaḥ | kāmukalokaḥ kathayati sattrāgāreṣu bhuñjānaḥ
35 upasaṃhṛtānyakarmā dhanavarmā narmadāṅghiyugalasya | sakalasamarpitasampadyadupetaḥ pādapīṭhatvam
36 yadupanato nayadattaḥ sāgaradattasya madhyamaḥ putraḥ | prīṇayati madanasenāṃ vidhāya pitṛmandiraṃ riktam
37 yallīlārpitacaraṇau mañjaryā bhaṭṭaputranarasiṃhaḥ | paritoṣaṃ vrajati paraṃ mṛdu mṛgan pāṇiyugalena
38 yanniḥśeṣitavibhavo dīkṣitabhavadevaputraśubhadevaḥ | nirbhatsito 'pi nojjhati kesarasenāgṛhadvāram
39 anyā api kāmijanaṃ sādhāraṇayoṣito yadākramya | vidadhati karpaṭaśeṣaṃ vilasitametattavopadeśānām
40 hīnānvayajanmāno guṇahīnā rogiṇo nirākṛtayaḥ | upasevitā mayāpi prakaṭīkṛtarāgasauṣṭhavaṃ puruṣāḥ
41 mātaḥ kiṃ vidadhāmo hatadhāturvāmatābhiyogena | nāsādayāma iṣṭaṃ nijatanupaṇyaprasārakeṇāpi
42 tatkuru mātaranugrahamabhidhatsva mamāpi dehino bhogyān | teṣāṃ ca veśaceṣṭitamanasijaśarajālapātanopāyān
43 iti giramudīrayantīṃ sapremāmṛśya pāṇinā pṛṣṭhe | ruciravaco vikarālā rucirākṛtimālatīmūce
44 ayameva dahyamānasmaranirgatadhūmavartikākāraḥ | cikurabharastava sundari kāmijanaṃ kiṃkarīkurute
45 ayameva te kṛśodari mandollasitabhruvibhramādhāraḥ | adharīkaroti dhīrān madhurasmitasubhagavīkṣitaviśeṣaḥ
46 iyameva vadanakāntī ratikāntākūtamatitarāṃ kurute | śrutipathamapyupayātā niyataṃ tava kāmināṃ manasi
47 iyameva daśanapaṅkī rucirācirakāntikāntisamakāntiḥ | utpādayati nitāntaṃ tava manmathadāhavedanāṃ puṃsām
48 idameva samullapitaṃ līlāvati vijitaparabhṛtadhvānam | tava niḥśeṣabhujaṅgavyākarṣaṇasiddhamanvavaccitram
49 idameva makaraketananiketanaṃ stanayugaṃ tavābhogi | bhogavati bhogasādhanamaparopāyagraho vyarthaḥ
50 idameva bāhuyugalaṃ mṛṇālatanusundaraṃ tavābhogi | kasya na janayati madanaṃ kanakāṅgadabhūṣaṇaṃ sutanu
51 ayameva madhyadeśaḥ kandarpādeśakaraṇacaturaste | prakṛśo 'pi śarīrabhṛto daśamīṃ prāpayati manmathāvasthām
52 iyameva romarājiḥ saṃkalpajacāpayaṣṭiguṇaśobhām | dadhatī vidadhāti tava smarasāyakaśalyaviklavān yūnaḥ
53 idameva pṛthulajaghanaṃ kaladhautaśilātalābhiramaṇīyam | tava taruṇavaśīkaraṇaṃ yatisaṃyatināśakāri karabhoru
54 idameva tavoruyugaṃ rambhāgarbhopamaṃ manohāri | vada sundari nābhimataṃ madanajvaratāpaśāntaye kasya
55 yauvanakalpataroste kanakalatāvibhramaṃ suvṛttam idam | jaṅghāyugalaṃ necchati kāmaphalaprāptaye ka iva
56 nirjitadāḍimarāgaṃ vijitasthalakamalinīvilāsamidam | tava caraṇasarojayugaṃ kasya na mānasamalaṃkurute
57 hepayati vāraṇendraṃ haṃsaṃ hasati prayātamidameva | tava līlāvati lalitaṃ yūnāṃ hṛdayāni maśnāti
58 tadapi yadi te kutūhala-mavadhānaṃ saṃvidhāya tanumadhye ākarṇaya kathayāmi svabuddhivibhavānusāreṇa
59 svīkuru tāvatprathamaṃ nṛpasevakabhaṭṭasūnumatiyatnāt | svādhīnāmativipulāṃ yadi sampadamīhase sutanu
60 pratyāsannagrāme svayaṃ prabhuḥ pitari nityakaṭakasthe | bhaṭṭasutaścintāmaṇirākṛṣṭo bhavati putri niyamena
61 śṛṇu tasya cāruhāsini veṣagrahaṇaṃ ca ceṣṭitaṃ caiva | nipatati ca yathā tūrṇaṃ priyasurabhikusumaśarāsanaprasare
62 sthūlasthāpitacūlakapañcāṅgalamāttrakeśavinyāsaḥ | lambaśravaṇaniveśitakarapatrikaghaṭitadaśanapaṅgiśca
63 karaśākhāśritamudrikacāmīkarakaṇṭhasūtrikābharaṇaḥ | parimṛṣṭagātvakuṅkumakiṃcitpiñjaritavasanasaṃvītaḥ
64 pravilambikusumadāmakagalamaṇḍanajātarūpakṛtaśobhaḥ | antarniviṣṭasikthitatauruṣkikakhuśśikādicaraṇataḥ
65 nānāvarṇaviveṣṭitabahaladaśāpāśabaddhatatakeśam | ekasmindalavīṭakamaparasminsīsapatrakaṃ karṇe
66 ucccaṇḍakanakagarbhitakuṅkumapiñjaritavastiparidhānam | sthūlatarakācavartakamālāṃ ca gale dadhānena
67 vṛścikarañjitakararuhakaramūlanibaddhaśaṅkhacakreṇa | prathamavayastvaṃ bhajatā tāmbūlakaraṅkavāhinānugataḥ
68 śreṣṭhivaṇigviṭakitavapradhānaraṅgasya sumahato madhye | śūlāpālasthāpitakatipayavadhrorupīṭhikāsīnaḥ
69 utsaṅgārpitakhaṅgairayathātathabhāṣibhirmahauddhatyam | bibhrāṇairanujīvibhiradhiṣṭhitaḥ pañcaṣaiḥ puruṣaiḥ
70 caturatarasevakārpitapṛṣṭhaparikṣiptapūrvadehāṃśaḥ | antarvidhṛtāvelaprocchūnakapolakalitakaraparṇaḥ
71 anapekṣitaprasaṅgaḥ punaḥ punaḥ paṭhati sonnatabhrūkaḥ | gāthāślokaprāyaṃ bhāvitacetā yathātathādhītam
72 vismayalolitamauliḥ pārśvagatāntāḍayatrasāvegāt | hākaṣṭasādhuvādairantarayati parasubhāṣitaśravaṇam
73 idamukto rahasi ruṣā tātena nṛpo nṛpeṇa tāto 'pi | iti piturāviṣkurute mahībhṛtaḥ praṇayaviśvāsau
74 pattracchedamajānan jānanvā kauśalaṃ kalāviṣaye | prakaṭayati janasamāje bibhrāṇaḥ pattrakartarīṃ satatam
75 brahmoktanāṭyaśāstre gīte murajādivādane caiva | abhibhavati nāradādīn prāvīṇyaṃ bhaṭṭaputrasya
76 vasunandacitradaṇḍaka-muktāyudhakhaṅgadhenubandheṣu | vrajati purato 'sya niyataṃ bhārgavatāṃ paraśurāmo 'pi
77 vātsyāyanamayamabudhaṃ bāhyaṃ dūreṇa dattakācāryam | garaṇayati manmathatantre paśutulyaṃ rājaputraṃ ca
78 yaḥ prārthito 'pi yatnātkavacaṃ rādhāsuto dadāti sma | avicintitavasuvarṣastyāgaguṇaṃ hasati tasyāyam
79 prapalāyanaikahṛdaye yo vikramamātanoti hariṇe 'pi | siṃhasya tasya śauryaṃ trapākaraṃ bhaṭṭaputrasya
80 ākheṭake 'pi kautukamastyeva jayaśca cañcale lakṣye | bhaṭṭabhayena na khelati bhaṭṭasutaḥ kiṃ tvatiprakaṭam
81 iti nijasevakanigaditarāmaṇikāvacanajanitaparituṣṭyā | antarmudito brūte māmeṣa khalīkarotīti
82 katamatkatamallagnaṃ prasthānaṃ kā ca nartakī bhadrā | ṣiṅgaṭake kā nṛtyati kohalabharatoditakriyayā
83 kīdṛk tvaṃ layamārge dhavalukaracite ca bhāṇake kīdṛk | prekṣaraṇakādāvevaṃ pṛcchati nṛttopadeśakaṃ yatnāt
84 sumanomālāṃ kaṇṭhātsādaracetā dadāti nartakyai | apanīya satāmbūlakamanavasare sādhuvādaṃ
85 bhujapatanagāttrasaṃsthitilālityodvahanapārśvavalitāni | anayaiva nirmitāni sthānakaśuddhiśca cāturasrayaṃ ca
86 pravibhaktairbhāvarasairabhinayabhaṅgayā parikramaiścitaiḥ | rambhāmapyatiśete kimutetaramartyanartakīlokam
87 ityapasārakaviratā-vaviratamutsnāyukaṇṭhamatyuccaiḥ | varṇayati bhāvitātmā lakṣitapadamāttrayā pātram
88 prāyeṇa bhaṭṭatanayo bhavatīdṛśaveṣaceṣṭito vatse | taṃ madanavāgurāntaḥ pātayasi yathā tathā brūmaḥ
89 caturā prāgalbhyavatī paracittajñānakauśalopetā | yojyā tasmindūtī vakroktivibhūṣitā prayatnena
90 sa upetya tayāvasare tāmbūlaṃ sumanasaśca dattvettham | abhidhātavyaḥ sundari makaradhvajadīpakairvacanaiḥ
91 janmasahasropacitaiḥ puṇyacayairadya phalitamasmākam | yattvaṃ nayanānandana nayanāvasaraṃ sameto 'si
92 cāṭukramamanurāgaṃ praṇayaruṣau virahajanitaśokārtim | prakaṭayati vāraramaṇī naṭīva śikṣābhiyogena
93 pravayasi yauvanaśālini hīnakule satkulaprasūte ca | rogavati dṛḍhaśarīre samacittā yoginaśca gaṇikāśca
94 upacaritāpyatimātraṃ prakaṭavadhūḥ kṣīṇasampadaḥ puṃsaḥ | pātayati dṛśaṃ vrajataḥ spṛhayā paridhānamātre 'pi
95 itthaṃ dṛḍhataravāsitamanasāṃ puṃsāṃ tvasāmprataṃ purataḥ | veśavilāsavatīnāmaśarīraśaravyathākathanam
96 kevalamagaṇitalāghavadūraparityaktadhīratābharaṇā | mukharayati māṃ durāśādagdhasakhī tena kathayāmi
97 hṛdayamadhiṣṭhitamādau mālatyāḥ kusumacāpabāṇena | caramaṃ ramaṇīvallabha locanaviṣayaṃ tvayā bhajatā
98 kṣaṇamutkaṇṭakitāṅgī kṣaraṇamulvaṇadāhavedanāvasthā | cakṣaṇamupajātākampā svedārdravapuḥ kṣaraṇaṃ bhavati
99 muhuravibhāvitahāsyā muhurujjhitadhīrabhāvamatyuccaiḥ | roditi gāyati ca punaḥ punaśca maunāvalambinī bhavati
100 patati muhuḥ paryaṅke muhuraṅke parijanasya muhuravanau | kisalayakalpitatalpe muhurambhasi muhuranaṅgasaṃtaptā
101 mahiṣīva paṅkadigdhā haṃsīva mṛṇālavalayaparivārā | subhaga mayūrīvāsau bhujaṅgavidveṣiṇī jātā
102 kadalī candanapaṅkaḥ paṅkeruhanīrahāraghanasāram | śaśadharaśaśadharakāntaṃ śāntyai no madanahutabhujastasyāḥ
103 apasāraya ghanasāraṃ kuru hāraṃ dūra eva kiṃ kamalaiḥ | alamalamāli mṛṇālairiti vadati divāniśaṃ bālā
104 saṃkalpairupanītaṃ tvāmantikamullasanmanovṛttiḥ | dṛḍhamāliṅgati paścātsvabhujāpīḍena yāti vailakṣyam
105 kusumāmodī pavanaḥ pikakūjitabhṛṅgasārtharasitāni | iyamiyatī sāmagrī ghaṭitā vidhinaiva tadvināśāya
106 abalāṃ balinā nītāṃ daśāmimāṃ makaraketunā rakṣa | āpatpatitoddhṛtaye bhavati hi śubhajanmanāṃ janma
107 no gṛhṇanti yathārthā arthijanairnigaditā giraḥ prāyaḥ | mālatyā guṇaleśaṃ śṛṇu dhṛṣṭatayā tathāpi kathayāmi
108 āsphālayato nūnaṃ dhanuratanoḥ kausumaṃ rajaḥ patitam | saṃhṛtya sā sumadhyā viśvasṛjā nirmitā tena
109 upahasati girisutāyā lāvaṇyaṃ yena satatalagnena | na dravatāmupanītaṃ bhogīndravibhūṣaṇasya dehārdham
110 śaśadharabimbārdhagatāṃ chāyāmiva saiṃhikeyavadanasya | alipaṭalanīlakuṭilāmalakāvalimalikasaṃnidhau vahati
111 sarasijamasthiraśobhaṃ vibhramarahitaṃ ca maṇḍalaṃ śaśinaḥ | kena sametu samatvaṃ hṛdayapriya mālatīvadanam
112 alirupari tadīkṣaṇayorbhāntvāsaugandhyasūcitaviśeṣaḥ | nipatati karṇāmburuhe nirguṇatāpyavasare sādhvī
113 bibhrāṇe 'ruṇimānaṃ sahajaṃ jitabandhujīvarucimadhare | yadalaktakavinyasanaṃ tattasyā maṇḍanakrīḍā
114 citvamidaṃ yatkṛśatā tasyā valiparigṛhītamadhyasya | athavā no vidhivihitā mahatāpyapanīyate tanutā
115 āstāmaparastāva-ttasyāḥ smaravasatipṛthutaranitambaḥ | ślathayati kapilamunerapi dṛkpathapatitaḥ samādhānam
116 tasyā rambhāvapuṣo rambhopamamūruyugalamavalokya | makaradhvajo 'pi sahasā nijasāyakalakṣyatāṃ yāti
117 jaghanabharālasayātā no yātā sā vilocanaprasaram | tiṣṭhati tena manohara śarajanmā brahmacaryeṇa
118 yadi kathamapi madhumathanaḥ paśyati tāmasamabāṇasarvasvam | tadasārabhārabhūtaṃ lakṣmīvapururasi vinihitaṃ manute
119 yadi patati sā kathaṃ cidvīkṣaṇaviṣayaṃ harasya tadavaśyam | tribhuvanamaśivaṃ kurute vāmetaradehabhāgamāsādya
120 saundarya tattādṛśamaśeṣayoṣidvilakṣaṇaṃ sṛjataḥ | yanniṣpannaṃ dhātustanmanye kākatālīyam
121 sahajavilāsanivāsaṃ tasyā vapuranabhivīkṣamāṇasya | manye nākādhipateḥ sahasramapi cakṣuṣāṃ viphalam
122 śithilayatu kusumacāpaṃ kṣipatu śarān bāṇadhau manojanmā | saṃsārasārabhūtā vicarati bhuvi mālatī yāvat
123 vātsyāyanamadanodayadattakaviṭavṛttarājaputtrādyaiḥ | ucchrusitaṃ yatkiṃ cittattasyā hṛdayadeśamadhyāste
124 bharataviśākhiladattilavṛkṣāyurvedacitrasūtreṣu | patracchedavidhāne bhramakarmaṇi pustasūdaśāstreṣu
125 ātodyavādanavidhau nṛtte gīte ca kauśalaṃ tasyāḥ | abhidhātuṃ yadi śakto vadanasahasreṇa bhogināmīśaḥ
126 parigaladālolāṃśukamapayantraṇamupari mālatī rabhasāt | nipatati nāpuṇyavatāṃ ratilālasamānasā rahasi
127 ratirasarabhasāsphālanacalavalayaninādamiśritaṃ tasyāḥ | tatkālocitamaṇitaṃ śrutipathamupayāti nālpapuṇyasya
128 itthamabhidhīyamānaḥ śubhamadhye yadi bhavedudāsīnaḥ | evaṃ tato 'bhidheyaḥ saṃdarśitakopayā dūtyā
129 kiṃ saubhāgyamado 'yaṃ yauvanalīlābhirūpatādarpaḥ | sahajapremopanatāṃ mālatikāṃ na bahu manyase yena
130 na gaṇayati yā kulīnān draviṇavataḥ śāstravedinaḥ praṇatān | sā bhavadarthe śuṣyati kusthānaniveśitaṃ dhiganurāgam
131 kamalavatī tīvrarucau bahubhasmani śambhuśirasi śaśilekhā | sā ca tvayi paśukalpe yadabhiratā tena me kṛśatā

398 paryastamitānaṅgo vyapagatanidraḥ kṣapāckṣayākāṅgī | grāmoṣitaḥ prahīraṇo niṣpratipattiḥ sthito 'dya sakhi manujaḥ
399 śṛṇu sakhi kautukamekaṃ grāmīṇakakāminā yadadya kṛtam | suratasukhamīlitācī mṛteti bhītena muktāsmi
400 aviditadeśaprakṛteḥ śaṭhātmakāddavidagdhato 'smābhiḥ | anubhūto rājasutādapi bhāṇḍaviḍambanākleśaḥ
401 priyasakhi lokasamakṣaṃ nagaraprabhuraṇā haṭhena nītāsmi | evaṃ bandhakadātudviguraṇārthaprārthane kuto 'nyāyaḥ
402 ākarṣantī jaghanaṃ vrajasi yathā vikṣatā nakhaistilaśaḥ | manye tathopabhuktā tvaṃ kerali dākṣiṇātyena
403 adhare binduḥ kaṇṭhe maṇimālā kucayuge śaśaptakam | tava sūcayanti ketaki kusumāyudhaśāstrapaṇḍitaṃ ramaṇam
404 iti śṛṇvannuṣasi giro nirvṛttaniśābhiyogagaṇikānām | so 'pi yathākriyamāṇaṃ pravidhātuṃ nirjagāma kartavyam
405 suracitarāgopacitisvīkṛtamanasastayā samaṃ tasya | yauvanasukhamanubhavato jagāma saṃvatsaraḥ sārdhaḥ
406 viśrambhakathāḥ kurvanvicarannudyānavedikāpṛṣṭhe | sahacarakarasaktakaraḥ sundarasenaḥ kadā cittu
407 sthūlaghanatantusantatitānitanānāmbarāvaraṇam | yaṣṭiprāntaniyantritadalavīṭakakutapatumbakaprāyam
408 buṭitacaraṇatvasaṃgatavisphuṭitābhyaktapādamalinatanum | tvaritagatilekhavāhakamārādāyāntamadrāccīt
409 pratyāsannībhūtaṃ krameṇa paurandariḥ parijñāya | sākūtamanā ūce vayasya hanumānayaṃ prāptaḥ
410 avanitalalīnaśirasā kṛtanatinā tena vinihitaṃ bhūmau | utkṣipya jhaṭiti lekhaṃ sundara iti vācayāmāsa
411 svasti śrīkusumapurātpurandaraḥ sundaraṃ samabhidhatte | antajṛmbhitaśokagrasto 'vispaṣṭavarṇapadam
412 kulamakalaṅka na gaṇitamavadhīritamagrajanmanāmucitam | nāpekṣitamavagītaṃ śaṭhasevitavatrtmani tvayā patatā
413 vaṃśe 'kuṭilagatīnāṃ dvijihvatādoṣarahitacaritānām | aparavināśaratānā-mutpannaḥ kathamasi bhujaṅgaḥ
414 kva puroḍāśapavivita-vedapadodgāragarbhavadanaṃ te | kva ca madirāsavavāsita-vāravadhūmukharasāsvādaḥ
415 ka kuśavipāṭanajanmā sahasoditavedanācamatkāraḥ | ka ca dāsīratasaṅgaranirdayanakharacātiḥ prītyai
416 ka vetānaladhūmaccobhitanayanāmbudhautavadanatvam | kaca gaṇikānirbhartsanaśokabharāyātabāṣpasalilaughaḥ
417 ka vaṣaṭkāradhvānaḥ ṣaṭkarmavibhūṣaṇaṃ śravaṇapūraḥ | kva ca sādhāraṇavanitāratimaṇitākarṇanautsukyam
418 vācāryapratanulatātāḍanasaṃccobhasambhavaḥ kampaḥ | kva ca kupitavāralalanāniṣvarapādaprahāraviṣahitvam
419 kva hariṇacarmāvaraṇaṃ smṛtiśāstraniveditaṃ vrataṃ carataḥ | kva ca paṇyastrīgāva-spṛṣṭāmbaradhāraṇeṣu bahumānaḥ
420 samidhāmeva cchedana-mabhyastaṃ śaiśavātsamārabhya | śaṭhavanitādharakhaṇḍana utpannaṃ kauśalaṃ kuto bhavataḥ
421 śuśrūṣaraṇameva guroḥ pariśīlitamacalacetasā satatam | kuṭilamatayo bhujiṣyāḥ kathaṃ tvayārādhitā nipuṇam
422 āmnāyapāṭha eva sphuṭatarapadasauṣṭhavaṃ tava khyātam | prakupitaveśyānunaye kva śikṣitaṃ vacanacāturyam
423 athavā kiṃ kriyate 'sminnavadātakule 'pi labdhajanmānaḥ | sadasaṃstutā bhavanti prāgupacitakarmadoṣeṇa
424 tvayi viniveśya kuṭumbaṃ paralokahitārjanaikanihitātmā | sthāsyāmīti samīhitamanudivasaṃ tadvisaṃvaditam
425 ityavadhṛtalekhārthe sundarasene vidheyasammūḍhe | āryāmagāyadanyaḥ svāvasare gītiparikaritām
426 viṣayatimirāvṛtādaraṇāmavaṭe patatāmadṛṣṭamārgāraṇām | puṃsāṃ gurujanavacanadravyaśalākāñjanaṃ śaraṇam
427 udvejayati tadātve sukhasampatti karoti pariṇāme | kaṭukauṣadhaprayogo gurunigaditakāryaniṣṭhuraṃ ca vacaḥ
428 labdhvātha vacovasaraṃ mitramavādītpurandarāpatyam | punarapi na hi khidyante priyajanahitabhāṣaṇe santaḥ
429 agaṇitasahacaravacaso durvyasanamahāvdhimagnavapuṣaste | manyuvyathitasya pituryadi paramavalambanaṃ vacanam
430 nijavaṃśadīpabhūtaḥ kṛtacaritālaṃkṛto mahāsattvaḥ | sundara samprati tātaḥ spṛṣṭo duṣputradoṣeṇa
431 putrābhāvaḥ śreyān duḥsutatā putriṇaḥ kulīnasya | antastāpayati bhṛśaṃ saccaritakathāprasaṅgeṣu
432 sāṃvyavahārika eva prāyo loke guṇonnatā niyatāḥ | yena tu sutena jananī vandhyātvaṃ ślāghate sa pāpīyān
433 viphalaṃ śāstrajñānaṃ gurugṛhasevāpi nopakārāya | viṣayavaśīkṛtamanaso nyāyyaṃ panthānamutsṛjataḥ
434 jīvanneva mṛto 'sau yasya jano vīcya vadanamanyonyam | kṛtamukhabhaṅgo dūrātkaroti nirdeśamaṅgulyā
435 no parihartuṃ viṣayāḥ śakyāḥ satyaṃ tathāpi nipuṇadhiyaḥ | abhidheyatāṃ na gacchantyapavādaviśeṣitābhidhānasya
436 guruparicaryā jāyā kulodgatā snigdhabandhusamparkaḥ | brāhme karmaṇi saktirlokadvayasādhanaṃ sudhiyām
437 sulabhā tasya vibhūtistasya guraṇā yānti jagati vistāram | bahu manute taṃ sujanastasmai spṛhayanti bāndhavāḥ satatam
438 nāsādayati sa ekaḥ satsevitamārgataḥ pariskhalanam | maṇḍayati so 'nvavāyaṃ sa nivāsaḥ śarmaṇāmaśeṣāṇām
439 sa bhavati vinayādhāro yuktāyukte vivekitā tasya | vṛddhopadeśavācaḥ śravaṇodaratarpaṇaṃ sadā yasya
440 prāktanakarmavipākaḥ kṣudrāsu śarīriṇāṃ yadāsaktiḥ | āyatanaṃ tu sukhānāṃ saṃsārabhuvāṃ kulodgatā rāmā
441 nivispe nirvispā mudite muditā samākulākulite | pratibimbasamā kāntā saṃkuddhe kevalaṃ bhītā
442 yāvadvāñchitasuratavyāyāmasahāviruddhasaṃbhāṣā | cittānuvṛttikuśalā puṇyavatāmeva jāyate jāyā
443 sadbhāvapremarasaṃ valayāvaliśabdaśaṅkitā nibhṛtam | vidadhānāṅgasamarpaṇamunmīlitakusumasāyakākūtā
444 hā hā kimuddhatatvaṃ śroṣyati kaścidgatavapa svairam | nikaṭe parivārajano vismṛta eva smarāturasya tava
445 iti huṃkṛtisaṃvalitairāyāsaniveditārthapadavākyaiḥ | dviguṇīkaroti kulajā nāyakakarmāṇi mohanaprasare
446 itthamudīritavācaṃ suhṛdamavocatpurandarasya sutaḥ | samupasthitajīvasamā-viyogabhayakampito vacanam
447 tātādeśe 'laṅghaye hāralatāvirahapāvake tīvra | vidhivaśavartini maraṇe no vidyaḥ kāryapariṇāmam
448 anapekṣitadhanalābhāṃ snehaikanibaddhamānasāṃ dayitām | daivākṛṣṭo muñcati ghaṭito vā lohavajjrakaṇikābhiḥ
449 atha kṛtagamanaviniścitirabhimatarāmāṃ cakāra viditārthām | sāpi tamanuvavrāja prastutayāvaṃ śucākulitā
450 āsādya vaṭasya talaṃ bāṣpapayaḥkaraṇacitākṣipadamāgrām | viśnitacaraṇavihāro hāralatāmabhidadhāti sma
451 ā kṣīravato vṛkṣā-dā salilādvā priye priyaṃ yāntam | anuyāyāditi vacanaṃ tena tvamito nivartasva
452 kiṃ kurmī daivahatāḥ prabhavati yasmin kṛśodari prasabham | premagranthicchettā guruśāsanasāyako nirāvaraṇaḥ
453 na draviṇacayaprāptinaikāśrayaparicayo na cāṭuguṇaḥ | na svāmisamādeśo nākāravilobhanaṃ na ca khyātiḥ
454 hetustava pravṛtterasmāsu tathāpi daivavaśāt | īdṛkko 'pyanubandho yasya vipāko pratīkāraḥ
455 paruṣaṃ yadabhihitāsi praṇayaruṣā śaṅkitaṃ ca narmaṇi vā | sudati na tatsmaraṇīyaṃ durbhāṣaṇakīrtanoddhāte
456 tava hṛdaye hṛdayamidaṃ vinyastaṃ nyāsapālanaṃ kaṣṭam | yatrāttathā vidheyaṃ sthānabhraṃśo yathā na syāt
457 atha viratavacodayitaṃ bāṣpabharakliṣṭavarṇapadayogāt | iti kathamapi hāralatā saṃmūrchitavastubhāratīmūce
458 aviśuddhakulotpannā dehārpaṇajīvikā śaṭhācaraṇā | vāhaṃ rūpājīvā kva bhavantaḥ ślāghanīyajanmaguṇāḥ
459 yattu viṣayāvalokanakutūhalābhyāgatena viśrāntam | iyato divasānasmin tanme parajanmakṛtaśubhasya phalam
460 gurusevāṃ bandhujanaṃ svadeśavasati kalatramanukūlam | anuṣaṅgadṛṣṭaparicita āsthāṃ pravidhāya kaḥ parityajati
461 yauvanacāpalametadyanmādṛśi bhavati kautukaṃ bhavatām | yattu sukhamanavagītaṃ tasya sthānaṃ nijā dārāḥ
462 te madhurāḥ parihāsāstā vakragiraḥ sa vāmatāsamayaḥ | no hṛdaye kartavyā rahasi comāthinā bhavatā
463 lāghavato yanmanasaḥ praṇayādvā yattavācaritam | pratikūlaṃ tattra mayā nāthāñjalireṣa viracito mūrdhni
464 duḥsaṃcārā mārgā dūre vasatirvisaṃpṭulaṃ hṛdayam | guṇapālita tava suhṛdā bhavitavyamato 'pramattena
465 hṛdayadvaya ekatvaṃ yāte yūnorviyogajaṃ kleśam | anubhavatorapareṇa prasaṅgataḥ paṭhyate pathyā
466 anyonyasudṛḍhaceṣṭitasadbhāvanehapāśabaddhānām | vicchedakaro mṛtyurdhīrāṇāṃ vā paricchedaḥ
467 atha tacchravaṇānantaramāssva sukhaṃ dayitike vrajāmīti | abhidhāya yāti mandaṃ sundarasene vivartitagrīvam
468 vaṭaśākhālambibhujāṃ śvasitoṣṇasamīraśuṣyadadharamukhīm | paryastāṃ bibhrāṇāṃ tanmārgavilokanānimeśadṛśam
469 dolāyamānaveṇīṃ tiryaggatakaṇṭhabhūṣaṇaviśeṣām | galadadhuvāripūrṇāṃ patitāṃśukabhāganiḥsahāṅgalatām
470 rundhānāmiva hṛdayaṃ sphuṭaditarakareṇa kucayugāśrayiṇā | pariśeṣitāṃ vilāsairutsṛṣṭāṃ jīvalokakartavyaiḥ
471 aṅgīkṛtāṃ vipattyā vaśīkṛtāṃ marmaghaṭṭanairviṣamaiḥ | hāralatāmaparisphuṭamantaḥ parikṛṣyamāṇabhāratyā
472 mā mā tāvadyāta kṣaṇamekaṃ yāvadeṣa niṣkaruṇaḥ | vanagulmairna tirohita ityabhidadhatīṃ jahuḥ prāṇāḥ
473 atha paścātsamupetaṃ papraccha purandarātmajaḥ pathikam | dṛṣṭā śokavyathitā nivartamānāṅganā bhavatā
474 sa uvāca vaṭataroradha urtyāṃ patitā viniścalāvayavā | tiṣṭhati vanitā nānyā nayanāvasaraṃ gatāsmākam
475 iti tadvacanāśmahato vihvalamūrtiḥ papāta bhūpṛṣṭhe | utthāpitaśca suhṛdā so 'bhidadhe tena śokadīnena
476 bhavatu kṛtārthastātastvamapi sumitrāssva sāmprataṃ prītaḥ | samakālameva muktā pāpena mayāsubhiśca hāralatā
477 hā hā hāva hato 'si dhvastā līlā vilāsa kiṃ kuruṣe | uccchinnā vicchittibhrama vibhrama daśa diśo nirādhāraḥ
478 kilakiṃcita gaccha vanaṃ moṭṭāyitamaśaraṇatvamupayātam | kuṭṭamita pravrajyāṃ gṛhāṇa bibboka viśa bhuvo vivaram
479 lalitamanāthībhūtaṃ vihṛtasya na vidyate gatiḥ kvāpi | śaśadharabimbadyutimuṣi yātāyāmantakāntikaṃ tasyām
480 vinivṛttya yāmi dagdhuṃ madvirahe muktavallabhaprāṇām | bhavatu varākyāstasyāḥ saptārcidardānamātramupakāraḥ
481 gatvātha tamuddeśaṃ yasmin sā pañśvabhāvamāpannā | vilalāpa muktanādaṃ viluṭhan bhuvi sahacareṇa dhṛtamūrtiḥ
482 ete vayaṃ nivṛttā muñca ruṣaṃ dehi kopane vācam | uttiṣṭha kimiti tiṣṭhasi bhūmitale reṇurūṣitaśarīrā
483 vinimīlya dṛśau kasmādapratipattyā sthitāsi śubhavadane | tvadavāritagamanavidheraparādhitayā na me yogaḥ
484 nākādhipatipurandhrīrabhibhavituṃ tvayi divaṃ prayātāyām | satsvapi śareṣu pañcasu nirāyudhaḥ sāmprataṃ madanaḥ
485 vaśvakavṛttā veśyā ityapavādo janeṣu yo rūḍhaḥ | apanīto 'sau nipuṇaṃ tvayā priye jīvamokṣeṇa
486 vandyaḥ sadrūta ekastripurāntakanandano mahāsenaḥ | hṛdayaṃ yasya spṛṣṭaṃ na manāgapi vāmalocanāpremṇā
487 manye 'bhīṣṭaviyogaṃ nimeṣamapi duḥsahaṃ samavadhārya | hariṇā vakṣasi lakṣmīvidhṛtā gaurī hareṇa dehārthe
488 ayi lokapāla sā bhuvi lalāmabhūtā tayā vinā śūnyam | viśvamiti kiṃ na cintitamātmasthānaṃ priyāṃ nayatā
489 bhagavan hutavaha mā mā lāvaṇyasamudrasāramuddhṛtya | kathamapi vihitāṃ dhātrā dhakṣyasyenāṃ jagadbhūṣām
490 iti vilapantaṃ bahuvidhamavadhīrya suhṛt purandarasya sutam | kāṣṭhaiviracayya citāṃ tāmakarodagnisāgaṇikām
491 tasminniddhahutāśanavinipatane kṛtamatau śucākulite | manasi sphuritāmāryāṃ papāṭha kaścitprasaṅgena
492 anumaraṇe vyavasāyaṃ strīdharme kaḥ karoti savivekaḥ | saṃsāramuktatyupāyaṃ daṇḍagrahaṇaṃ vrataṃ muktā
493 śrutvā sundarasenaḥ sacivamavocayapetavaiklavyaḥ | pratibodhitaṃ mano me dhīreṇānena yuktamupadiśatā
494 kṣaraṇadṛṣṭanaṣṭavallabhajanmajarāvyādhimaraṇaparibhūte | āvartini saṃsāre kaḥ kuryādāgrahaṃ sumatiḥ
495 yātu bhavān kusumapuraṃ vayamapyantyāśrame samāśrayaṇam | aṅgīkurmī 'vidyāprahāraṇasaṃsiddhaye vihitam
496 so 'vadadabhijātajano bālyātprabhṛti tvayā ca na viyuktaḥ | saṃnyasanabuddhimadhunā kathamujjhati viṣayaniḥspṛhaṃ suhṛdam
497 evamiti so 'bhidhāya sthirayatiniyamaistapodhanairjuṣṭam | guṇapālitena sahitaḥ sundaraseno jagāma vanam
498 evaṃ bhavantu veśyāḥ svārthekadṛśo vyapetasadbhāvāḥ | abhilaṣitaviṣayasiddheḥ kā hānistadapi yuṣmākam
499 ramaṇahṛdayānuvartanacaturacatuḥṣaṣṭikarmakuśalānām | na spṛśati tattvacarcā paṇyavadhūnāṃ vidagdhacetāṃsi
500 valitaptacivagatisthitivegaiścodanānuvṛttyā ca | rāgasparśena vinā viśati manaḥ sādināṃ turagaḥ
501 gandho 'pi kutaḥ premṇaḥ parabhṛtahārītagṛhakapotānām | ujjvalayantyasameṣu virutaviśeṣaistathāpi te yūnām
502 āhitayuktāhāryaḥ samyaksakalaprayoganiṣpattyā | bhāvavihīno 'pi naṭaḥ sāmājikacittarañjanaṃ kurute
503 ye 'pi dhanacाyadoṣaṃ paśyanti jaḍā vilāsinīśleṣe | praṣṭavyāste bhavatā kimakṛtakaśipuvyayā dārāḥ
504 na ca lābha eka eva pravartane kāraṇaṃ manuṣyeṣu | rāgādayo 'pi santi vaiśikaśāstrapraṇetṛbhiḥ kathitāḥ
505 kā vā vibhūtirāptā sundarasenāttayā tapasvinyā | tadvirahakuliśabhinnā mumoca yā jīvitaṃ kṣaṇārdhena
506 uttamataruṇaprakṛtiḥ pulakādikasūcitānyatanusaktiḥ | sphuṭasaṃnihitavibhāvo nivāryate kena śṛṅgāraḥ
507 antaḥkaraṇavikāraṃ guruparijanasaṃkaṭe 'pi kulaṭānām | budhyanti tadabhiyuktā bhrūbhaṅgāpāṅgamadhuradṛṣṭena
508 anyā vihāya patigṛha-mavicintitakulakalaṅkajanagarhāḥ | rāgoparaktahṛdayā yānti digantaṃ manuṣya āsajya
509 apamānaḥ pativihito guruparikaratīvratā gṛhe 'pi daurgatyam | śīlakṣataye yāsāṃ tāsāmapi rāgato 'nyanarasaktiḥ
510 yā apyacalitavṛttā bhartuḥ paricaraṇatatparāḥ pramadāḥ | tā api rāgavimuktāstiṣṭhantyaucityamātreṇa
511 tasmādastvabhigamanaṃ vividhanimittaṃ nivāryate kena | nijaparapaṇyastrīṇāṃ rāgādhīnaṃ tu hṛdayanirvahaṇam
512 evaṃvidhadṛṣṭāntairupapattiyutaistathedṛśairvākyaiḥ | anyairapi cāṭupadairāvarjitamānasaṃ gamyam
513 vihitasvāpavibodhaṃ kiṃcitprakaṭīkṛtaśramaglānyā | utpāditajṛmbhikayā parirabhya ghanaṃ niśāpagame
514 vighaṭitapuṭamudradṛśā vilokya kakubhaḥ sadīrghaniḥśvāsam | vaktavyamiti bhavatyā rajani khale kiṃ prayātāsi
515 abalā viṣaheta kathaṃ dṛḍhaśaktimanuṣyaratirasaprasaram | madanajanito 'nurāgo na vidadhyādyadi balādhānam
516 dhanyā cakrāhvavadhūḥ priyatamasaṃghaṭanasamayasamprāptyā | śaśinā viyujyamānā kumuddhati kṣīṇapuṇyāsi
517 vikasitasurabhimanoharasaṃsthānaṃ sarasakusumamaprāptam | na karoti tathā pīḍāmāsvāditavicyutaṃ yathā bhṛṅgayāḥ
518 vijñāpayāmyatastvāṃ racitāñjalimaulinā vidhāya natim | paricārakajanamadhye gaṇanīyāhaṃ prasādena
519 atha dīpitarāgāṅgairapahastitalābhadikkramopacitaiḥ | mṛdubhiścittānugatairupacāraiḥ pātitasya viśvāse
520 avalokito 'si lampaṭa kimapi vadan karṇasaṃnidhau nibhṛtam | śaṅkarasenādhāvyā adya mayā jālamārgeṇa
521 mālatyā saha keliṃ vidadhāsi sakhī mameti na virodhaḥ | yattu ciraṃ snigdhadṛśā paśyasi tāṃ tattra me śaṅkā
522 tvāmāgatā na vīkṣitumanubadhya na yācitaḥ prayatnena | āhūya vada kimarthaṃ tāmbūlaṃ grāhitā kamaladevī

655 vyapagatakoṣe rāgiṇi yāti layaṃ pānamātralābhahṛtā | kṣudrā madhukarikābje na tu gaṇikā cintitasvārthā
656 yāsāṃ kāyaryāpekṣā sakaṭākṣānirīkṣaṇe 'pi veśyānām | darśanamātracubhitairvavyante tāḥ kathaṃ puruṣaiḥ
657 kleśāya durgatānāṃ mānastutigāvabhaṅgavinyāsam | gaṇikābhinayacatuṣṭayamākṛṣṭyai svāpateyapuṣṭānām
658 kiṃ dhakṣyati bhūyo 'pi jvalanastaṃ tādṛśaṃ kulāṅgāram | yo dahyate 'virāmaṃ viraktadāsītiraskāraiḥ
659 gṛhametadīśvarāṇāṃ kāntāraṃ duṣpraveśamanyeṣām | pūtkṛtamidamudbhūjayā na mālatī kāmasattūdānaparā
660 iti coditagṛhaceṭīnigaditakaṭukāctarānyakṛtalakṣyāḥ | ākarṇayato vāco daivopahatasya tasya marmabhidaḥ
661 evamabhidhīyamāno no budhyati yadi paśurnarākāraḥ | tadidaṃ sundari vācyaḥ prathitavacasā tvayā kāmī
662 prīyata eva tavopari hṛdayaṃ me kiṃ tu gurujanādhīnā | mātṛvacotikramaṇaṃ na samarthā saṃvidhātumaham
663 arhasi tāvadatastvaṃ gantumitaḥ katipayānyapi dināni | punarapi bhavataiva samaṃ bhoktavyaṃ jīvalokasukham
664 nirvāsite tu tasmin yaḥ kāmī pūrvamujjhito bhuktā | tasya prāptavibhūteryuktiriyaṃ bhinnasaṃdhāne
665 upavanalīlāviharaṇahāvojvalamañjulasya saha tena | varṇanamitivṛttasya smarajavikārāśva vīkṣite tasmin
666 idamupavanamatidhanyaṃ nirbharamāliṅgitaṃ surabhilakṣmyā | matskandhāpitapāṇirbabhrāma sa yatra jīvitādhīśaḥ
667 sakhya ito bhramarakulattāsitayā priyatamo mayā sahasā | vakrībhavatpayodharamupagūḍho dhīrasītkāram

669 mayi jātādhikarāgo balavati madane sahāyasāmayyā | kāntaḥ pallavaśayane no tṛptimagādviviktakāryeṣu

671 spṛhaṇīyo 'yamaśokaḥ spṛṣṭo yadvallabhena hastena | asmadavataṃsakārthaṃ nūtanadalapallavān vicārayatā

673 utthāpaya mānarase dayitaṃ caraṇāgranipatitaṃ tūrṇam | atyākṛṣṭaṃ vuṭyati sudṛḍhamapi premabandhanaṃ mūḍhe
674 tiṣṭhannapi yātasamaḥ kiṃ tena nivāritena sakhi paśunā | yāmīti niṣprakampaṃ viniḥsṛtā yasya mādhave vāraṇī

681 kiṃ nirmito 'si dhāvā navo 'paraḥ kimu vasantaguṇa eṣaḥ | kusumaśarapūrṇatūṇaḥ kimutābhavadanya eva kandarpaḥ
682 no paśyasi yadi kakubhaḥ pracurojjvalasurabhikusumaramaṇīyāḥ | parabhṛtakūjitamiśrānna śṛṇoṣi yadi dvirephabhaṃkārān
683 gandhaṃ yadi no labhase vāsitadigvyoma sumanasāṃ hṛdyam | anubhavasi yadi sparśa no śītaladākṣiṇātyapavanasya
684 rasanendriyaikaśeṣaḥ parasaṃcāryo janena paribhūtaḥ | nārhasi tato 'pi muktā nijāśrayaṃ gantumanyato nitarām

686 punarantarjalamagno māmupagamyāvibhāvitaḥ sahasā | uccickṣepa sahāsaṃ hāsitasaṃnihitaparivāraḥ
687 saṃsaktārdrāvaraṇaṃ jaghanaṃ naḥ paśyatastadā tasya | prathamākāṅgākūtaṃ bheje sambhogaśṛṅgāraḥ
688 kālapradeśaveśavyāpārasthitiviśeṣaghaṭanābhiḥ | cirarūḍho 'pi hi yūnāṃ navatvamupanīyate rāgaḥ
689 sādaramarpayato 'bjaṃ govaskhalanāparādhinastasya | sakhyaḥ smarāmi sahasā vilackṣatāṃ kliṣṭahasitasya

693 nīvivimocanabandhau muhurmuhuḥ keśapāśaviśleṣaḥ | svādharadaśanagrahaṇaṃ bālakaparicumbanaṃ ratotsukatā

695 ekībhāvaṃ gatayorjalapayasormivacetasośca tathā | vyatirekakṛtau śaktiham̐sānāṃ durjanānāṃ ca
696 atipelavamatiparimitavarṇaṃ laghutaramudāharati śaṇṭhaḥ | paramārthataḥ sa hṛdayaṃ vahati punaḥ kālakūṭaghaṭitamiva
697 yena tadā māmūce parijanamutsārya vivṛtanaṭamanyuḥ | darśitahitasvarūpaḥ parapīḍākaraṇapaṇḍitaḥ prakhalaḥ
698 aviditaguṇāntarāṇāṃ ko doṣaḥ prāntadeśavāsānām | svādhīnakuṅkumā api yadvidadhati bahumatiṃ nīle
699 ka mahītalarambhā tvaṃ nyakkṛtacandraprabhā svadeharucā | citralatā kka varākī nīcairupasevitā voṭā
700 yasyārthe na vigaṇitāḥ prahvātmāno mahādhanāḥ kulajāḥ | so 'dya hṛdayena tasyāṃ tvayi tiṣṭhati bāhyavṛttena
701 tāmeva samācaraṇāṃ sadasadbhāvapravartitāṃ nipuṇaiḥ | vindanti tatra kuśalāḥ snehavirūkṣāprabhedena
702 tava tu virūḍhapremṇastatkarmavivecanaṃ manovṛttiḥ | nāruhatīti mayaivaṃ niveditaṃ pāricityena
703 iti durjanāhiniḥsṛtavāgviṣadūṣitasamastavapuṣo me | īrṣyārujaḥ pravṛddhāśvirarūḍhapraṇayakhaṇḍanaprabhavāḥ
704 laghuhṛdayatayā yasmāddarbhāṣitavajjrapātavihatānām | vaktṛtviśeṣavitarko na spṛśati prāyaśo manaḥ strīṇām
705 priyamapi vadan durātmā kṣipati vipatsāgare duruttare | āsādya prāṇabhṛto mṛtaye parileḍhi jihvayā khaṅgaḥ
706 hitamadhurākṣaravāraṇīvyavahāramanupraviśya tallīnam | saralā durāśayānāmupaghātaṃ phalata eva vindanti
707 parasaṃtāpavinodo yavāhani na prayāti niṣpattim | antarmanā asādhurna garaṇayati tadāyuṣo madhye
708 divasāntānabhinandati bahu manute teṣu janmano lābham | ye yānti duṣṭabuddheḥ paropatāpābhiyogena
709 vikasitavadanaḥ prakhalaḥ protphullavilocano yathā bhramati | manye tathā na jātaḥ sadahitakaraṇaśramo vandhyaḥ
710 śaṭhamṛgayuḥ kusṛttiśarairajñātapratividhānasādhumṛgān | abhyastalakṣavedho nighnanna pariśramaṃ vrajati
711 anukūlavarapurandhriṣu puruṣāṇāṃ baddhamūlarāgāraṇām | nayati mano duḥśīlaḥ kusumāstro hīnapātreṣu
712 sāvaraṇaṃ vrajato 'nyāṃ kautukadṛṣṭyā prasaṅgato dayitān | buddhāpi vidagdhadhiyo vartante nāṭyadharmeṇa
713 satyaṃ premaṇi vṛddhe vyathayati hṛdayaṃ manāgapi skhalitam | avadhṛtanijamāhātmyāstadapi na dhīrā vimuhyanti
714 svacchandaṃ pivatu rasaṃ bhrāntvā bhrāntvā vanāni kusumeṣu | anubhūtaguṇaviśeṣaḥ punareṣyati mālatīṃ madhupaḥ
715 mālatyā guṇavattāṃ samyagno vetti madhukarastāvat | anubhavameti na yāvatsumanontarasaṃgamāsvāde
716 komalamānakadarthāṃ bhajamāno bhajati dīptatāmadhikām | saṃcālyamānadāruḥ pāvaka iva suprabhaḥ snehaḥ
717 yaḥ punaratikopānalasaṃtāpavaśena dūramākṛṣṭaḥ | kācamariṇaḥ sa khalu yathā pariṇāme khaṇḍakhaṇḍamupayāti
718 vetanalobhādbahavaḥ sevyante sauṣṭhavena pañcajanāḥ | viśrāmyati yattra manaḥ sa tu duṣprāpaḥ sahasreṣu
719 manvādimunivarairapi kālatrayavedibhiḥ sudurjeyam | tatsukṛtaṃ yasya phalaṃ rabhasāgatavallabhāśleṣaḥ
720 yāte 'pi nayanamārga preyasi yasyāḥ smṛtirvyalīkeṣu | manye tāṃ prati niyataṃ kuṇṭhitaśarapañśvakaḥ madanaḥ
721 jīvyata eva kathaṃ ciddhigvṛttimimāṃ mahadbhiravagītām | vijahāti yanna gaṇikā tadvāñchitaramaṇalābhalobhena
722 kaṇṭakinaḥ kaṭukarasān karīrakhadirādiviṭapatarugulmān | upabhuñjānā karabhī daivādāpnoti madhuramadhujālam
723 kā śrīrapraṇayivaśā kā vilasitayo manobhavavihīnāḥ | ko dharmo nirupaśamaḥ kiṃ saukhyaṃ vallabhena rahitānām
724 svācchandyaphalaṃ bālyaṃ tāruṇyaṃ rucitasuratabhogaphalam | sthaviratvamupaśamaphalaṃ parahitasampādanaṃ ca janmaphalam

726 adhunānutāpapāvakamadhyagatā pacyamānasarvāṅgī | niṣphalajanmaprāptirjīvāmyucchrāsamātreṇa
727 sthāneṣu yeṣu yuṣmatsaṃgatayā krīḍitaṃ ciraṃ dhṛtyā | tāni khalu vīkṣamāṇā bhavāmi kaṇṭhasthitaprāṇā
728 anyavaśena visaṃjñā kṛtabhūṣā yanvasūvasaṃcārā dārumayīva pratimā vidadhāmi viḍambanā bahvīḥ
729 yadi nāmodarabharaṇaprāptyai kurute 'nyapuṣpasaṃśleṣam | tadapi na puṣṭirbhṛṅgayā apivantyā āravindamakarandam
730 āstāmaparo lokaḥ krīḍāpekṣī parāpadi prītaḥ | vyasanārṇave patantī na vāritā parijanenāsmi
731 ki vā bahubhiḥ kathitaiḥ samprati niyame tu niyamitā buddhiḥ | sthāsyāmi saṃniyuktā bhavagahe preṣyakāryeṣu
732 iti nettrādivikārairvaśamupanītaṃ pralīnadhairyāṅgam | māragrahābhibhūtaṃ parimṛṣṭaprāṅanirākṛtismaraṇam
733 prādurbhūtariraṃsaṃ kṣaṇe cakṣaṇe jatrudeśagatadṛṣṭim | pakvāmramiva vimokṣyasi pūrvavadācūṣya subhru niḥśeṣam
734 svaśarīrāmiṣadigdhaṃ vakrasmitadṛṣṭipātavāgbaḍiśam | prakṣipyākṛśya jaḍaṃ sphuraṇena vivarjitaṃ suparipuṣṭam
735 hastadvayāntarāgatamupacāraparivyayena saṃskṛtya | bhuktā yāvanmāṃsaṃ tyakṣyasi carmāsthiśeṣitaṃ matsyam
736 śṛṇu suśroṇi yathāsmin kalaśeśvarapādamūlamañjaryā | pravarācāryaduhivā rājasutaścarvitaśca muktaśva
737 āsīcchrīsihabhaṭo nāmnā nṛpatirmahīyasāṃ praṣṭhaḥ | tasyātmajo 'dhitasthau niveśanaṃ devarāṣṭrasaṃbaddham

739 mūrdhavibhāgasaṃsthitabṛhadambaracīrakeśasaṃyamanaḥ | alpācchagāvarāgo dhanakuṅkumaliptakarakiśāgraḥ
740 siddhārthabījadanturalalāṭatilakopayuktatāmbūlaḥ | śravaṇaniveśitakuṇḍalaṭīṭibhakaprāyakandharābharaṇaḥ
741 keyūrasthānagatasvarṇāvṛtamanvagarbhajatuguḍakaḥ | maṇibandhanavinyastapracalāṅkurajātarūpamaṇimālaḥ
742 dhṛtavevadaṇḍa ūrṇakapariveṣṭitasāsidhenukanitambaḥ| mṛdutarapaṭikāvaraṇaḥ śabdolbaṇacurcurāṅkacaraṇavaḥ
743 gambhīreśvaradāsyāṃ lagnaḥ kila tava vayasyako vīraḥ | prāpsyati sāpi durāśāvarṣavitayena yanmayā prāptam
744 darśayati diśaḥ phalitā amṛtagabhasti kare 'vatārayati | suradevi candravarmā nirvastukavākprapañcena
745 tvāmanuyāntaṃ samprati paśyāmi kuraṅgi vasuṣeṇam | sunirūpitā bhaviṣyasi viṣamā guḍajihvikā tasya
746 carvayati jalaṃ yo 'sau hariṇi haro dhūrtatābhimānena | likhati śataṃ daśavṛddhyā sa nimagnastaralikāvarte

750 straiṇaṃ paśyati yuktyā sākāṅga varjitānyajanadṛṣṭiḥ | kumudini mama hṛdayagataṃ bhavitavyaṃ vyājaliṅginānena
751 paśyatyadṛśyamāno nirīkṣito vīkṣate parāṃ kakubham | būte kiṃ citsaspṛhamabhiyukto bhavati kīlitadhvānaḥ

757 agropaviṣṭanartakavāṃśikagātṛprakāśayuvatigaraṇaḥ | śreṣṭhipramukhavaṇigjanaḍhaukitatāmbūlakusumapaṭavāsaḥ

759 tāmbūlakaraṅkabhṛtā sandaṃśagṛhītavīṭikāgrahaṇe | īṣatspṛṣṭaṃ kurvanmandaṃ khaṭakāmukhena vāmena

762 jaya deva parabalāntaka gurucaraṇārādhanaikakṛtacitta | paravanitājaghanāsana dāridryatamaḥ pracaṇḍakarajāla
763 raṇadhīravaṃśabhūṣaṇa guruvasudhādevapūjanaprahva | śaraṇāgatābhayaprada hitavāndhavabandhujīvamadhyāhna

766 śrīphalabhukpatravṛto vigraharasiko vimuktatsattaratiḥ | rājyasthiti na muñcati hṛtalakṣmīko 'pi tava vipakṣagaṇaḥ
